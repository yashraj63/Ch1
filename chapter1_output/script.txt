aur FY twenty six mein in companies ne milkar lagbhag 7 hazaar se zyada positions kam kiye

matlab agar thirty five days mein apko naya project nahi mila, to demotion, pay-cut, ya sidha aapko company se nikala bhi Ja sakta h. Wahi Infosys or dusre bde players ne bhi andar hi andar "assessment failures" aur restructuring ke naam par hzaro logon ko bahar ka rasta dikha diya.
Jinme se kai employees silent layoffs ka bhi shikar hue
Boardrooms mein baithe un top executives ka logic us waqt bohot simple tha: Jo code likhne mein ek mid-level human developer ko hafton lagte hain, wo naya AI tool kuch seconds mein generate kar dega. Unhe laga ki is automation se unka profit margin double ho jayega. Lekin unhe ye andaza nahi tha ki automation ki ye jaldbazi unhe IT history ke sabse mehenge u-turn ki taraf le jaa rahi thi.
